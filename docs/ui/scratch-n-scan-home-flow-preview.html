Crispy Pink Salt Potato Chips

A homemade version inspired by kettle-style pink salt chips, using real potatoes, controlled oil, and clear cooking steps.

Time35–50 min
MethodAir fry / bake
BasePotatoes
  • 2 large russet or Yukon gold potatoes
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons avocado, olive, or sunflower oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon pink salt, plus more to taste
  • Optional garlic powder or smoked paprika
  • Cold water for soaking slices
  1. Wash potatoes and slice very thin with a mandoline or crinkle cutter.
  2. Soak slices in cold water for 20 to 30 minutes to remove starch.
  3. Drain and pat slices completely dry.
  4. Toss with oil and pink salt until lightly coated.
  5. Bake at 400°F for 15 to 25 minutes, or air fry at 350°F for 10 to 16 minutes.
  6. Cool for several minutes so the chips crisp before serving.
